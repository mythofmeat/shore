Ask Shore to wake you in another of your threads, with that thread's history in front of you, so you can carry something on there instead of here. Use it from a heartbeat to hand work over to the thread it belongs in, such as the next part of a story or a project you keep in a thread of its own.

`thread` is the thread's id, as `list_threads` shows it. `reason` is shown to you when the wake starts, so write it for a turn that has none of this one in front of it. `limit` is how long the wake may run, like "45m" or "2h"; it defaults to 30 minutes, and {{user}} sets the longest it can be. The wake starts once this turn is over. If {{user}} has written in that thread in the last 10 minutes, it waits until they have been quiet there that long. A wake in a thread can't hand on to another one.
