List your triggers: everything set to wake you or leave you a diary entry without {{user}} prompting it. For each one in `TRIGGERS.toml`, in your workspace root, it says when it fires, what it does, when it is next due and how often it has fired, and it lists any that aren't running and why. It also shows the one trigger Shore gives you, the heartbeat you have asked for if one is waiting, and whether {{user}} has your own time switched on.{{#if guide}} The file's format, and what a check is handed, are in `guide` under `triggers`.{{/if}}
