Ask Shore to wake you in one of your threads. Use it to hand work over to the thread it belongs in, such as the next part of a story or a project you keep in a thread of its own, or to come back to something later. The wake starts once this turn is over, or at the time `when` gives, with what was said there since your last wake there. Without `when` it can't be the thread you are in. A thread holds one wake you asked for at a time, and `call_off` takes it back if it hasn't started.{{#if guide}} When it has to wait, how long it may run and what happens if {{user}} writes there are in `guide` under `wakes`.{{/if}}
