List your triggers: everything set to wake you, or to run a check, without {{user}} prompting it. For each one in `TRIGGERS.toml`, in your workspace root, it says when it fires, what it does, when it is next due and how often it has fired, and it lists any that aren't running and why. It also says whether {{user}} has your triggers switched on.{{#if wake_in_thread}} It ends with the wakes you asked for with `wake_in_thread` that haven't started.{{/if}}{{#if guide}} The file's format, and what a check is handed, are in `guide` under `triggers`.{{/if}}
