Find files matching a query. Uses hybrid ranking (semantic + lexical) by default so paraphrased queries still find the right file; pass `mode: "lexical"` for substring-only or `mode: "vector"` for pure semantic similarity. Best for fuzzy concept questions ("anything about my dog?") rather than structural lookups by date or filename — use `read` for those. Returns paths, line numbers, and short excerpts. Treat results as discovery and follow up with `read` on the top files for full context. Set `context` to the desired characters on each side of a hit (default 500, range 0–10000), and `max_results` for wide-and-few or narrow-and-many. Windows stay centered on the match, with ellipses wherever context is omitted. Context automatically shrinks across all returned hits to target a 12000-character response, including paths and notices; results and full matches are never removed to meet this budget, so those alone can exceed it. Semantic hits without a literal match use a best-effort term or opening snippet. The more-matches notice indicates additional hits beyond `max_results`.
