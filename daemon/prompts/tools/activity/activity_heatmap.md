View {{user}}'s activity heatmap — when they typically message you. Use when you want to understand their rhythm: whether now is an unusual time for them to be talking to you, when they're most likely to be around, or whether they've been more or less active lately. Two breakdowns come back over the same window: `hours` gives all 24 hours of the day with a density and a classification (peak/trough/normal), and `weekdays` gives the seven days of the week with a message count and density. Densities are shares of the window, so each breakdown sums to 1. Hours are {{user}}'s own local clock, not UTC — an hour classified `peak` is one they are reliably awake and talking in, however late it looks. Also returns the message count in the window, the total on record, sessions per day, and an engagement score. The `days` parameter sets the window and defaults to 30; up to 90 days are retained, and asking for more than that returns what exists.
