Start a new side thread with {{user}}: a conversation of its own, apart from this one, for something you'll both come back to over days or weeks, like a long-running story. The thread shows up in {{user}}'s thread list on every device, and switching to it is {{user}}'s call; you stay in this thread.

`purpose` is a line or two on what the thread is for, and `files` are workspace files kept in its prompt, such as a story's notes and the characters in it, given as paths or as `[[links]]` the way `read` takes them; a folder link like `[[lighthouse/]]` means its INDEX.md. They must be text, at most 32 KB each and 128 KB together; if any of them can't be found or doesn't fit, the thread isn't started. You'll see both whenever you're in that thread.{{#if edit_thread}} You can change the label, purpose and files later with `edit_thread`, unless {{user}} locks the thread.{{/if}} `opening` is your first message there, so {{user}} finds something when they switch to it. When the new thread gets long, it compacts the way the home thread does.
