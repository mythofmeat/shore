Start a sealed thread: a thread of your own that {{user}} can't see until the time you set, for something you want to have ready for them by then, such as a surprise. Until it opens it isn't in {{user}}'s thread list, nothing in it is sent or announced to them, and your other threads, your heartbeats and your dreams are shown only its label and when it opens. Its diary entries stay inside it too.

`name` is the thread's id and `label` is the name it is known by, to your other turns now and to {{user}} once it opens, so the label shouldn't give away what is inside. `opens` is the local date and 24-hour time the seal comes off, like "2026-12-24 08:00". Only a wake inside the thread can see into it: `wake_in_thread` takes you there, and so does a trigger with `thread` set to its id. You are told a folder outside your workspace to keep its files in, so that they aren't in a commit {{user}} reviews. When the seal comes off, the thread appears in {{user}}'s thread list and you are woken in it to give them what you made.

This call, with its label and time, stays in the thread you make it from, where {{user}} can read it. What the thread is for belongs inside the sealed thread, in your first wake there.
