Start a sealed thread: a thread of your own that {{user}} can't see until the time you set, for something you want to have ready for them by then, such as a surprise. Until it opens, your other turns are shown only its label and when it opens, so the label shouldn't give away what is inside. This call, with its label and time, stays in the thread you make it from, where {{user}} can read it, so say what the thread is for inside the sealed thread, in your first wake there.{{#if guide}} How to work inside it, and what happens when it opens, are in `guide` under `threads`.{{/if}}
