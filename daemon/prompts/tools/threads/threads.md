Your threads with {{user}}: list them, start one, or change one. `action` says which.

- `list`: each thread's id, label, purpose and files, who started it and when, when {{user}} last wrote there, whether it compacts, and whether {{user}} has locked it. It also says which thread is home and which one you're in.
- `start`: a new side thread, a conversation of its own apart from this one, for something you'll both come back to over days or weeks, like a long-running story. It shows up in {{user}}'s thread list on every device, and switching to it is {{user}}'s call; you stay in this thread. If any of `files` can't be found or doesn't fit, the thread isn't started.
- `start` with `sealed_until`: a sealed thread, one of your own that {{user}} can't see until that time, for something you want to have ready for them by then, such as a surprise. Until it opens, your other turns are shown only its label and when it opens, so the label shouldn't give away what is inside. It takes only `name`, `label` and `sealed_until`: this call stays in the thread you make it from, where {{user}} can read it, so say what the thread is for inside the sealed thread, in your first wake there.
- `edit`: change a side thread's label, purpose or files when they've gone stale. It works on any side thread, whoever started it, but not on the home thread, or on a thread {{user}} has locked. Give only what should change: an empty `label` or `purpose` clears it, and `files` replaces the thread's whole list. If any file can't be found or doesn't fit, nothing changes, the label and purpose included.{{#if guide}}

What each kind of thread sees, what a purpose and files do, how big the files may be and when a change to them shows up, and how a sealed thread is worked in and opens, are in `guide` under `threads`.{{/if}}
