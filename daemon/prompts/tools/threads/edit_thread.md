Change a side thread's label, purpose or files when they've gone stale. It works on any side thread, whoever started it, but not on the home thread, or on a thread {{user}} has locked. Give only what should change: an empty `label` or `purpose` clears it, and `files` replaces the thread's whole list. If any file can't be found or doesn't fit, nothing changes, the label and purpose included.{{#if guide}} When the change shows up, and how big the files may be, are in `guide` under `threads`.{{/if}}
