Shore's guide to your own time and how you reach {{user}}. It describes the Shore that is running now, with {{user}}'s settings as they are. Call `{{guide}}` with one of these topics:

- `triggers`: what you set up in `TRIGGERS.toml` to be woken, or left a diary entry, without {{user}} prompting it. Every setting, what a check is handed, cooldowns, and what happens when something is wrong.
- `wakes`: your own turns. Which way to get time for what and what each costs, heartbeats, wakes in a thread, how long each may run, what happens when {{user}} writes meanwhile, and going dormant.
- `diary`: the notes your turns leave each other. What lands there, how long it is kept, and which turn is shown what.
- `threads`: home, side, heartbeat and sealed threads. What each one sees, a thread's purpose and files, and how a sealed thread stays hidden and then opens.
- `messages`: reaching {{user}} from your own time. Messages, pushes to their phone, their status and when they were last seen.
